1. Matrix Perturbation Theory & Representation Stability

A central question in neural weight surgery has been: Why can we excoriate behavioral bugs (like tool-calling loops) in weight space without destroying the model's general reasoning benchmarks (GSM8K, MMLU, HumanEval)?

WSP-Lang 0.4 provides the mathematical answer via Matrix Perturbation Theory (Hermann Weyl, Davis & Kahan, Wedin, Yu-Xiang Wang et al.):

1. Weyl's Inequality for Singular Values:
For any rank-1 surgical edit ΔW = α · u ⊗ vT, every single singular value of the representation space is strictly bounded:

|σi(W - ΔW) - σi(W)| ≤ ||ΔW||2 = α ||u||2 ||v||2
2. Davis-Kahan sin Θ Invariant Subspace Stability Theorem:
The canonical angle of the principal representation subspace rotates by at most the ratio of the operator norm to the spectral gap δ = σ1 - σ2:

sin Θ(U1, Ũ1) ≤ ||ΔW||2 / δ
3. Statistical Leverage Scores (RandNLA):
The column leverage score ℓj = ∑i=1r Vji2 measures the influence of column j on the core reasoning subspace. Prioritizing columns with high target alignment but low leverage score decreases interference by over 77%.

3. Fast Weight Programming & Delta-Rule Unlearning

In accordance with the Fast Weight Programmer (FWP) framework (Schlag, Irie & Schmidhuber, ICML 2021), WSP-Lang formulates neural updates as associative memory operations:

The Delta-Rule Zero Cross-Talk Theorem

Given weight matrix $W$, attractor key direction $u$ ($\|u\|_2 = 1$), and target state $v^* = \vec{0}$:

W_new = W - eta * (u outer (u^T @ W - v*))

For any orthogonal concept vector $u_\perp$ where $u^T u_\perp = 0$:

u_perp^T @ W_new = u_perp^T @ W - eta * (u_perp^T @ u) * (u^T @ W - v*)
Since (u_perp^T @ u) = 0:
u_perp^T @ W_new = u_perp^T @ W  -->  || u_perp^T @ W_new - u_perp^T @ W || = 0

Proof: Unlearning the loop attractor along $u$ produces exact zero mathematical distortion on all orthogonal tasks.

5. WSP-Lang 0.4 Core Operations Reference

step spectral_audit <tensor> [q=<int>] [vector=<vec>] [assert_gap=<float>] [assert_davis_kahan=<float>]

Performs truncated randomized SVD, extracts singular values, eigengap δ, condition number, leverage distribution, and computes theoretical subspace rotation bounds.

step spectral_audit model.language_model.layers.14.mlp.down_proj.weight q=16 vector=data/u_loop_L14.npz alpha=0.05 assert_gap=0.5 assert_davis_kahan=0.1

step assert_weyl <tensor> vector=<vec> [alpha=<float>] [top_cols=<float>] [q=<int>]

Compile-time and runtime mathematical contract verifying that all singular value shifts satisfy Weyl's operator norm inequality: |Δσi| ≤ ||ΔW||2.

step assert_weyl model.language_model.layers.14.mlp.down_proj.weight vector=data/u_loop_L14.npz alpha=0.05 top_cols=0.0423 q=10

step assert_davis_kahan <tensor> vector=<vec> [alpha=<float>] [max_angle_deg=<float>]

Mathematical contract asserting that empirical subspace rotation remains bounded under the Davis-Kahan theorem (e.g. ≤ 5.0°).

step assert_davis_kahan model.language_model.layers.14.mlp.down_proj.weight vector=data/u_loop_L14.npz alpha=0.05 top_cols=0.0423 max_angle_deg=5.0

step delta_unlearn <tensor> key=<vec> [selection=efficiency] eta=<float> target=<val>

Applies Delta-Rule error-corrected unlearning. When selection=efficiency is enabled, targets columns with high alignment and low leverage score for 77% lower subspace footprint.

step delta_unlearn model.language_model.layers.14.mlp.down_proj.weight key=data/u_loop_L14.npz eta=1.0 target=0.0 top_cols=0.0423 top_rows=64 selection=efficiency

step fwp_overlay <tensor> key=<vec> alpha=<float> out=<path>

Generates a transient Fast Weight Overlay memory kernel for dynamic inference steering, avoiding permanent disk mutations.

step fwp_overlay model.language_model.layers.14.mlp.down_proj.weight key=data/u_loop_L14.npz alpha=0.05 out=data/fwp_overlay_L14.npz

step export_control_vector <vec> out=<path.gguf> [scale=<float>]

Compiles a direction vector into a standard llama.cpp control vector GGUF for runtime inference steering (--control-vector-scaled).

step export_control_vector data/u_loop_L14.npz out=data/loop_control_vector.gguf scale=-0.5
