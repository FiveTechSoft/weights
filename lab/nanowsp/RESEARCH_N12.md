# N12 — bounded reuse of discovered laws (2026-11-25)
Local Python execution reproduced N11: 99/99 laws, 46600/46600 reserved-symbol positives, 358904/358904 negatives, 15/15 ambiguous rejected, inconsistent and outside-family rejected.
N12 uses N11 discovery with length-4 outputs. For 64 seeded trials (random.Random(1212)), sample five distinct two-variable equality partitions from the seven possible partitions of four slots. Build four hidden guarded transformations linking consecutive partitions, each optionally swapping the two variables. Infer each from four positive observations on symbols 0..7. The learner searches compositions of at most two library calls, first discovering the two-step prefix, then three-step prefix, then four-step prefix by reusing learned macros. Unique expanded programs required at shortest call length. For the final comparison exclude the final macro itself. Cold control has the four primitives but no learned prefixes. Compare two-call search and a deterministic length-ordered cold enumeration capped at the warm final search's 42 candidate evaluations. Exhaustively check all seven two-variable equality partitions over distinct symbols 8..15.
Observed local execution: 64/64 warm successes, 0/64 cold two-call successes, 0/64 equal-final-budget cold successes; 256 primitive discoveries, 192 macro discoveries; 3584 positive and 21504 negative checks, all correct. Cold two-call examined 1280 total; warm final 2688 total; all three macro-discovery steps 5888 total (includes the final discovery, excludes repeated warm measurement and primitive discovery). No native weights constructed.
Limitations: exploratory, not preregistered. Tasks deliberately have a chain structure and incremental prefix examples. Warm learner has additional prior task data and search work; equal FINAL budget is not equal lifetime compute or data. Cold exhaustive length-four search could succeed; the truncated enumeration is not a strong optimized search baseline. All operations are symbolic, external and from a predefined family. The experiment demonstrates bounded compositional reuse, not open-ended invention, native neural generalization or RSI. Next: compare amortized cost over multiple held-out tasks, stronger cold baselines and compile discovered guarded programs to native weights.
