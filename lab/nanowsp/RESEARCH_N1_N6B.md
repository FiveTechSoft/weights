## Mathematical findings
For a strictly positive distribution p define L=log(p)-mean(log(p)). Softmax(L)=p. With two unused coordinates +/-sqrt((C-||L||²)/2), C>=||L||², the residual has zero mean and fixed squared norm C. Final LayerNorm gain sqrt(C/d+eps) preserves L under the tied centered-simplex readout. For a richer context use centered log probability ratio as correction, with corresponding anchor correction. GELU(x)-GELU(-x)=x implements linear residual maps exactly in real arithmetic; large-margin GELU conjunctions approximate discrete context selectors.
N6 retrieved U^T e in 64 coordinates: orthonormal projection preserves norm in the zero-sum character subspace but not the coordinate sum. LayerNorm subtracts the mean of the entire residual. N6b decodes back via U in 65 centered coordinates. Isolated change confirmed native fidelity; not a universal law of intelligence.
