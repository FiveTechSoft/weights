# N22 — relevance rather than identity alone
New corpus: GITenberg Pride and Prejudice ebook 1342 (Jane Austen), pin blob before evaluation. Same tokenizer, vocabulary, chronological 70/15/15 split as N21. Fit counts and classes only on first 70%. Tune KN discount {.75,.9,.95,.99} and last-class mixture {0,.1,.2,.4} on selection. Add lexical four-gram interpolated absolute discount .9 backing off to this strong trigram/class mixture; tune interpolation with fourgram via fixed discount grid {.5,.75,.9,.95,1} (1 means disabled explicitly). Use same baseline for all arms. Retrieve exact previous word at endpoints t-128..t-9; weights r = 2^(-lag/H) times product over preceding 1..3 offsets of (1+beta*similarity), similarity either exact identity or class match (exact counts once). H in {32,128,infinity}, beta in {0,1,3}, offsets fixed 3, modes exact/class. Tune alpha {4,16,32,64,128,infinity} jointly on selection. Compare separately tuned uniform (H=infinity,beta=0), recency-only, exact-context, class-context. Candidate selected among exact/class context with beta>0, not on acceptance. Controls permute weights among exact matches per target (same total weight); also evaluate uniform tuned and recency tuned. Gate: selection and acceptance gain >=.02 vs baseline and >=.01 vs best noncontextual retrieval (selected on selection), acceptance block-bootstrap CI lower >.01 vs baseline and >0 vs uniform, recency and shuffled weights. No tuning on acceptance. Report coverage, effective weight and all grid scores. Exclude first 132 tokens per slice to ensure all offsets valid. External statistical feasibility only; no semantic or neural claims. New author, not a zero-shot transfer test. If fails, do not compile this retrieval mechanism.
